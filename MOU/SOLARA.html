MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING

THE KACHOO
and
SOLARA INTERNATIONAL

This Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) is made and entered into by and between:

THE KACHOO, a media and publication company, operating under the KAZINE brand, with its principal office at Jenderal Sudirman Avenue, Lots 61–62, South Jakarta, Indonesia, hereinafter referred to as "Party One".

SOLARA INTERNATIONAL, a talent and model agency, with its principal office at [Solara Address], Indonesia, acting on behalf of its talents, hereinafter referred to as "Party Two".

Party One and Party Two are hereinafter individually referred to as a "Party" and collectively referred to as the "Parties."

1. Engagement & Campaign Overview

Party One hereby engages Party Two's talents to participate in KAZINE Vol. 1 Issue 3, themed "How Nature Helps Us Heal" with campaign tagline "Time to Heal", scheduled for release in September 2026. Party Two accepts such engagement on behalf of its talents under the terms and conditions set forth in this MOU.

  • Prudence Claire as Campaign Model sponsored by Choosic. Featured on the Early Page of KAZINE Vol. 1 Issue 3.
  • Ethan Matthijs Eedenrijk as Commercial Model for product Cho°L. Featured on the Product Section Page of KAZINE Vol. 1 Issue 3.

2. Scope of Services

The Talents agree to provide the following services during the term of this MOU:

  • Magazine Feature: Campaign Model to appear on the Early Page; Commercial Model to appear on the Product Section Page of KAZINE Vol. 1 Issue 3.
  • Professional Video Shoot: Participate in a professional video shoot as scheduled by Party One to support the KAZINE brand and sponsored products.
  • Live Instagram Content: Create and broadcast live content on Instagram as requested by Party One, up to the number of sessions mutually agreed upon.
  • Instagram Post Content: Create and publish static Instagram posts as requested by Party One, up to the number of posts mutually agreed upon.
  • Promotional Support: Actively promote KAZINE and the sponsored products (Choosic, Cho°L) across the Talents' personal social media channels, including but not limited to Instagram and X (Twitter).
  • Media Appearance: Make reasonable appearances and participate in promotional activities as requested by Party One for KAZINE.

Additional details regarding content specifications, posting schedules, and creative direction shall be mutually agreed upon in writing prior to execution.

3. Term

This MOU shall commence on August 8, 2026 and shall continue in full force and effect until September 13, 2026, or until all services and deliverables under this MOU have been fully performed, whichever is later.

4. Compensation

In consideration of the services rendered by the Talents under this MOU, Party One agrees to pay Party Two the following fees:

Service Rate / Fee
CAMPAIGN MODEL (Choosic)
Campaign Model (Main Fee) USD 3,500 / Rp. 62,000,000
Live Instagram (per content) USD 1,500 / Rp. 25,000,000
Post Instagram (per content) USD 900 / Rp. 15,000,000
COMMERCIAL MODEL (Cho°L)
Commercial Model (Main Fee) USD 1,000 / Rp. 18,000,000
Live Instagram (per content) USD 1,500 / Rp. 25,000,000
Post Instagram (per content) USD 900 / Rp. 15,000,000
AGENCY FEE (Solara International)
Agency Fee (per project) USD 1,200 / Rp. 20,000,000

Payment Terms: All fees are payable per project and per media content, as specified in the table above. Payment shall be made within thirty (30) days of invoice submission by Party Two, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

Party Two acknowledges that the Agency Fee is payable to Party Two as part of this MOU and shall be the responsibility of Party Two to remit to the Talents as per their internal arrangement.

5. Rights and Usage

5.1 Grant of Rights. The Talents grant to Party One a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license to use the Talents' names, likenesses, images, voices, biographical information, and any content created under this MOU (collectively, the "Talent Materials") solely for the purpose of promoting KAZINE, KAZINE Vol. 1 Issue 3, and the sponsored products Choosic and Cho°L.

5.2 Permitted Uses. Party One may use the Talent Materials in the following manners:

  • In KAZINE Vol. 1 Issue 3 (print and digital editions) — Campaign Model on Early Page, Commercial Model on Product Section Page.
  • On Party One's official website and social media platforms
  • In promotional materials, advertisements, and marketing campaigns for KAZINE Vol. 1 Issue 3 and sponsored products
  • In archival and press kit materials related to KAZINE Vol. 1 Issue 3

5.3 Restrictions. Any usage beyond KAZINE Vol. 1 Issue 3 or outside the scope of this MOU shall require additional written consent from Party Two and the Talents.

6. Talent Obligations

The Talents agree to:

  • Perform all services in a professional and timely manner, consistent with industry standards.
  • Follow the creative direction and reasonable requests provided by Party One regarding content creation and promotional activities.
  • Ensure that all content posted by the Talents in connection with this MOU complies with applicable laws and regulations, including but not limited to advertising disclosure requirements.
  • Follow the official X (Twitter) account @TheKachoo.
  • Retweet/share the official announcement post when KAZINE Vol. 1 Issue 3 is released.

7. Agency Obligations

Party Two agrees to:

  • Coordinate and facilitate the Talents' participation in all activities under this MOU.
  • Ensure the Talents' availability for all scheduled shoots, appearances, and content creation as mutually agreed.
  • Provide Party One with a single point of contact for all communications related to this MOU.
  • Handle all invoicing and payment matters on behalf of the Talents.

8. Party One Obligations

Party One agrees to:

  • Provide Party Two and the Talents with a clear brief and creative direction for all deliverables.
  • Feature the Campaign Model (Prudence Claire) on the Early Page and the Commercial Model (Ethan Matthijs Eedenrijk) on the Product Section Page of KAZINE Vol. 1 Issue 3.
  • Coordinate and schedule the professional video shoot and any related production activities.
  • Make timely payments of the compensation due under this MOU to Party Two.
  • Provide the Talents with complimentary copies of KAZINE Vol. 1 Issue 3.

9. Representations and Warranties

9.1 Party Two and Talents. Party Two and the Talents jointly represent and warrant that:

  • The Talents have the full right, power, and authority to enter into this MOU and to grant the rights granted herein.
  • The Talent Materials do not and will not infringe upon the rights of any third party, including but not limited to copyright, trademark, privacy, or publicity rights.
  • The Talents have no conflicting obligations that would prevent the full performance of this MOU.
  • Party Two has the full authority to represent the Talents and bind the Talents to the terms of this MOU.

9.2 Party One. Party One represents and warrants that:

  • Party One has the full right, power, and authority to enter into this MOU.
  • Party One shall use the Talent Materials solely in accordance with the terms of this MOU.

10. Confidentiality

Party Two and the Talents agree to keep confidential any non-public information related to KAZINE Vol. 1 Issue 3, including but not limited to creative concepts, unpublished materials, and internal communications, unless disclosure is required by law or approved in writing by Party One.

11. Non-Exclusivity

This MOU does not create an exclusive relationship between the Parties. The Talents are free to engage with other brands and projects, provided that such engagements do not conflict with the Talents' obligations under this MOU or negatively impact the KAZINE brand.

12. Termination

12.1 Termination for Convenience. Either Party may terminate this MOU by providing thirty (30) days' written notice to the other Party.

12.2 Termination for Cause. Either Party may terminate this MOU immediately upon written notice if the other Party materially breaches any provision of this MOU and fails to cure such breach within ten (10) days of receiving written notice thereof.

12.3 Effect of Termination. Upon termination, Party Two shall be entitled to payment for all services performed up to the date of termination, and Party One shall retain the right to use any Talent Materials created prior to the date of termination.

13. General Provisions

  • Independent Contractor: The Talents are independent contractors and not employees, agents, or partners of Party One. Nothing in this MOU shall be construed to create an employment relationship, partnership, or joint venture.
  • Governing Law: This MOU shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Republic of Indonesia.
  • Dispute Resolution: Any dispute arising out of or relating to this MOU shall first be resolved through good-faith negotiations between the Parties. If such negotiations fail, the dispute shall be submitted to mediation or arbitration in Jakarta, Indonesia, in accordance with the rules of the Indonesian National Arbitration Board (BANI).
  • Amendments: No amendment or modification of this MOU shall be valid unless made in writing and signed by both Parties.
  • Severability: If any provision of this MOU is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.
  • Entire Agreement: This MOU constitutes the entire understanding between the Parties with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior discussions, negotiations, and agreements.

14. Signatures

This MOU shall enter into effect upon signing by both Parties. The Parties acknowledge that they have read, understood, and agreed to be bound by all terms and conditions set forth herein.
