MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING (MoU)

THE KACHOO
and
[PARTY TWO NAME]

This Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) is made and entered into by and between:

THE KACHOO, a media and publication company, operating under the KAZINE brand, with its principal office at Jenderal Sudirman Avenue, Lots 61–62, Karet Semanggi, Setiabudi, South Jakarta 12930, Indonesia, hereinafter referred to as "Party One".

[FULL NAME OF PARTY TWO], a [Sponsor / Media Partner / Technical Support], with an address at [Address], hereinafter referred to as "Party Two".

Party One and Party Two are hereinafter individually referred to as a "Party" and collectively referred to as the "Parties."

1. Purpose

This MoU serves as a general framework of cooperation between Party One and Party Two for participation and contribution in KAZINE Volume 1, Issue 3, a lifestyle magazine publication produced by THE KACHOO under the theme "How Nature Helps Us Heal" and the campaign tagline "Time to Heal", targeted for release in September 2026. This MoU outlines the basic understanding, roles, and expectations of the Parties during the production, publication, and promotional period of KAZINE Vol. 1 Issue 3, without creating a legally binding partnership unless otherwise specified in a separate written agreement.

2. Scope of Collaboration

Party Two agrees to participate in KAZINE Volume 1 Issue 3 in the capacity of [Sponsor / Media Partner / Technical Support], including but not limited to:

  • Product, service, or creative contributions relevant to the theme of healthy living, mental & physical balance, and reconnecting with nature
  • Participation in editorial, visual, promotional, or production activities related to KAZINE Vol. 1 Issue 3
  • Use of likeness, brand assets, work, or intellectual contribution solely for KAZINE Vol. 1 Issue 3 purposes
  • Other mutually agreed activities relevant to the project

Specific deliverables, schedules (including the photoshoot session), and technical details may be further agreed upon separately in writing.

3. Duration

This MoU shall be valid for the duration of KAZINE Volume 1 Issue 3, commencing from:

Start Date: 3 August 2026
End Date: 13 September 2026

or for the full active period of KAZINE Vol. 1 Issue 3, including production, release, and related promotional activities, whichever period is longer.

4. Partner Contribution and Benefits

1) Contribution. Depending on the partnership category, Party Two agrees to provide the following:

  • Product/Brand Sponsor: send the sponsored product for use during the activity, with the detailed schedule coordinated during the photoshoot session or magazine release.
  • Other Sponsors (Venue, Hotel, Travel, Transportation, Service, etc.): post or quote-tweet the publication when the magazine is officially released.
  • Media Partner: promote The Kachoo ecosystem through at least 2 (two) posts/tweets on their respective media accounts.
  • Technical Support (MUA, Photographer/Studio, Transportation): attend and participate directly in the photoshoot session, and publish at least 1 (one) post about the activity.

2) Benefits. In return, Party One agrees to provide Party Two with the following benefits:

  • Dedicated Promotional Space: an allocation of 2 (two) to 4 (four) pages inside KAZINE Vol. 1 Issue 3 for product promotion, business profile, or portfolio.
  • Audience & Digital Exposure: cross-platform promotion through The Kachoo's digital media ecosystem and official social media.
  • Networking Opportunities: direct connection with young audiences, creators, influencers, and professionals in the creative industry.
  • Complimentary Products: 2 (two) of the newest products from KAZINE as a gift.

Any additional compensation, fee, or payment terms beyond the benefits above shall be mutually agreed upon and confirmed separately in writing.

5. Rights and Usage

Party Two grants Party One the right to use submitted works, images, likeness, name, brand, and related materials solely for:

  • KAZINE Volume 1 Issue 3
  • Editorial, promotional, marketing, and archival purposes related to the project

Any usage beyond KAZINE Vol. 1 Issue 3 shall require additional written consent from Party Two.

6. Responsibilities of the Parties

  • Each Party shall perform their respective roles in a professional and timely manner.
  • Each Party is responsible for their own expenses unless otherwise agreed in writing.
  • Party Two confirms that any submitted work, product, or material does not infringe upon third-party rights.
  • Follow Official Account: Party Two agrees to follow the official X (Twitter) account @TheKachoo.
  • Retweet Publication: Party Two agrees to retweet/share the post when KAZINE Vol. 1 Issue 3 is officially released.

7. Confidentiality

Both Parties agree to keep confidential any non-public information related to the project, including but not limited to creative concepts, unpublished materials, and internal communications, unless disclosure is required by law or approved in writing by the other Party.

8. Non-Exclusivity

Unless otherwise stated in a separate agreement, this MoU does not create an exclusive relationship between the Parties.

9. Termination

Either Party may terminate this MoU by providing written notice to the other Party. Termination shall not affect any obligations already fulfilled or contributions/benefits due for completed collaboration.

10. General Provisions

  • This MoU does not constitute an employment relationship, partnership, or joint venture.
  • Amendments to this MoU must be made in writing and agreed upon by both Parties.
  • This MoU shall be governed by the laws agreed upon by the Parties or, in the absence thereof, by mutual understanding.

11. Signatures

This MoU shall enter into effect upon signing by both Parties.

Party One

THE KACHOO

For and on behalf of KACHOO
