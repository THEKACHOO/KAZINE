Kazine is a lifestyle brand owned by The Kachoo, officially launched in early January 2026. Currently, Kazine offers two products, Choosic and Chozy, which are available through the Kazine Collection online store.

Kazine also publishes lifestyle magazines featuring articles on various lifestyle topics, along with promotional content created in collaboration with sponsoring partners. To date, Kazine has published two magazines as part of its Volume 1 series.

This Issue Vibe

KAZINE is preparing to release Vol. 1 Issue 3, featuring two new products: Chooler, our new tumbler, and Choffee, now introduced with a refreshed design for Kachoo's Coffee Box.

This issue embraces the theme of healthy living and reconnecting with nature. Readers can expect content centred on nature, outdoor adventures, wellness, and practical tips for living a healthier lifestyle.

We are currently seeking models, influencers, and actors whose personal brand aligns with the spirit of this edition. We also welcome collaboration opportunities with brands, destinations, venues, and services that complement our theme and vision.

Target Release: Late August or Early September 2026

Editorial and Technical Guidelines

1. All magazine editing and production are fully managed by the KAZINE Team.
2. All editorial content is created by the KAZINE Team. Sponsors are responsible for providing their promotional copy, which will be reviewed and refined by our editorial team before publication.
3. Product sponsors are required to provide their products as gifts for the featured talent and the Kachoo Team involved in the production. Service sponsors may discuss collaboration terms directly with the KAZINE Team.
4. Selected talent is required to participate in the official photoshoot. Further details are available here. Talent is also expected to either repost the magazine announcement or publish at least one social media post when KAZINE Vol. 1 Issue 3 is released.

Talent Requirements
  • No age restrictions. Applicants of all ages are welcome.
  • Open to everyone, including talent who has previously collaborated with KAZINE.
  • Should align with the concept of KAZINE Vol. 1 Issue 3 or be able to adapt to its theme.
  • All personalities, aesthetics, and styles are welcome, provided the content remains appropriate, avoids sensitive topics (SARA), and is relevant to the magazine's vision.
  • Must not be involved in any active public controversy. If a controversy arises after selection, the talent agrees to withdraw from this issue (while remaining eligible for future editions). KAZINE reserves the right to either select a replacement or leave the position unfilled.
  • Open to both agency-represented and independent talent.
  • Must be available for the scheduled photoshoot.
  • Must be willing to publish at least one promotional social media post upon the magazine's release.
How to Apply

Talent will be selected directly from the replies to our announcement tweet, so make sure your response stands out and showcases why you're a great fit for KAZINE.
