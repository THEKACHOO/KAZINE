Kazine Proposal

1. Introduction

The Kachoo is an integrated digital media and news company based in South Jakarta. The Kachoo oversees various innovative platforms including KAFKAPAGES, KAZINE, and KABOOM.

KAZINE is a lifestyle brand owned by The Kachoo, officially launched in early January 2026. KAZINE publishes a modern lifestyle magazine that combines inspiring articles, product catalogs, and collaborative promotional space with sponsoring partners.

Beyond the magazine, KAZINE has an exclusive product line including Choosic, Chozy, and two new product lines debuting in Issue 3: CHO°L (lifestyle tumbler) and Choffee (Kachoo's Coffee Box with refreshed packaging).

1. Pendahuluan

The Kachoo adalah perusahaan media digital dan berita terintegrasi yang berbasis di Jakarta Selatan. The Kachoo menaungi berbagai platform inovatif seperti KAFKAPAGES, KAZINE, dan KABOOM.

KAZINE adalah brand gaya hidup milik The Kachoo yang resmi diluncurkan pada awal Januari 2026. KAZINE mempublikasikan majalah gaya hidup modern yang memadukan artikel inspiratif, katalog produk, serta ruang promosi kolaboratif bersama mitra sponsor.

Selain majalah, KAZINE memiliki lini produk eksklusif seperti Choosic, Chozy, serta dua lini produk terbaru yang akan hadir di Issue 3: CHO°L (tumbler lifestyle) dan Choffee (Kachoo's Coffee Box dengan desain kemasan baru).

3. Partner & Sponsor Benefits

Joining as a partner for KAZINE Vol. 1 Issue 3 provides exclusive exposure for brands, media, and professionals through:

  • Dedicated Promotional Space: Allocation of 2 to 4 pages inside KAZINE Vol. 1 Issue 3 for product promotion, business profile, or portfolio.
  • Audience & Digital Exposure: Cross-platform promotion through The Kachoo's digital media ecosystem and official social media.
  • Networking Opportunities: Direct connection with young audiences, creators, influencers, and professionals in the creative industry.
  • Complimentary Products: Receive 2 newest products from Kazine as a gift.

  • Product Sponsor: Required to send the sponsored product during the activity (detailed schedule will be coordinated during the photoshoot session or magazine release).
  • Other Sponsors (Venue, Hotel, Travel, Transportation, Service, Etc): Required to post or quote-tweet when the magazine is officially released.

B. Media Partner

  • Required to promote The Kachoo ecosystem at least 2 tweets on their respective media accounts.

C. Technical Support (MUA, Photographer/Studio, Transportation)

  • Technical Team: Willing to attend and participate directly during the photoshoot session.
  • Publication: Required to make at least 1 post about the activity (e.g., behind-the-scenes documentation or when the magazine is released).
