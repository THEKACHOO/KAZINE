Photoshoot Guideline

First, the photoshoot schedule must be finalized and agreed upon in advance. Attendance on the scheduled day is mandatory, if a model is unable to attend, the session cannot be postponed, but the model will be required to make separate arrangements for a different schedule.

Prior to the shoot, models are expected to prepare a few reference photos to suggest for the magazine feature, which will later be evaluated and discussed by the writers (Out of Character (OOC)) within the GDM.

During the photoshoot, Kachoo's team will provide the photographer, who will make an initial selection of shots and share them directly or via a Google Drive link to choose the final shots.

The model's approval is required. Should the model disagree with the proposed selection, their prepared reference photos will be considered as alternatives.

Once the final selection is agreed upon, the photos will proceed to the editing phase, which is handled entirely by Kazine's team. Please note that once the editing session begins, all selected images are considered final and cannot be disputed or modified.

Additionally, models are permitted to share behind-the-scenes content or equipment teasers during the photoshoot. While posting is completely optional, any coverage provided will be calculated as an additional bonus added to the final payment.
